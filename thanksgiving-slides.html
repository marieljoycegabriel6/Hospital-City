What is Thanksgiving?

Thanksgiving is a day
to say thank you.

Who do we say thank you to? 🤔

SAY: “Thanksgiving is a day to say THANK YOU.” Everyone says “Thank you!” together. Ask: “Who do we say thank you to?” Let them guess (Mom! Dad!). ➜ NEXT: “How do people in America celebrate? Let’s fly to America and see!” Chinese: 謝謝 (xiè xie) = thank you; 感謝 (gǎn xiè) = thankful.

Let’s fly to America!

Loud and Soft!

🤫

Teacher whispers…

You say it softly.

📣

Teacher shouts…

You say it LOUD!

SAY: “Listen! When I whisper, you whisper.” (whisper “hello”) “When I shout, you shout!” (shout “HELLO!”) Try it once. ➜ NEXT: “Who do we say thank you to? Number one…” Chinese: 小聲 (xiǎo shēng) = soft; 大聲 (dà shēng) = loud.

Thankful words · 4 of 6

home

Thank you for my home! ❤️

SAY “home” twice; students repeat. Loud and Soft. Make a roof shape with your hands. Read together: “Thank you for my home!” ➜ NEXT: “In the morning, we leave home and go to…?” Chinese: 家 (jiā).

Thanks a million!

🙌

Do it!

Open your arms wide and say it big!

SAY “Thanks a million!” with arms open wide; students copy. A million is a very big number, so it’s a very big thank you. Practice: Teacher: “Here’s a gift!” Students: “Thanks a million!” ➜ NEXT: “Now say it to a friend!” Chinese: 非常感謝 (fēi cháng gǎn xiè); 一百萬 (yì bǎi wàn) = a million.
